A GAME ABOUT CLEAN NATURE IN SLOVENIA

Eco Bus Slovenia

Drive the green bus from the sea in Piran to the Alps in Bohinj. Carry people instead of cars and make the air clearer.

  1. Drive to the green bus stop zone and stop with the door next to the sign.
  2. Press Space (or Doors): passengers get off and new ones get on. Each stop also recharges your battery.
  3. Collect leaves 🍃 on the road for points and extra battery. If the battery is empty, the bus crawls.
  4. Watch the CO₂ meter below. The more people on board, the more CO₂ you save compared with everyone driving their own car.
